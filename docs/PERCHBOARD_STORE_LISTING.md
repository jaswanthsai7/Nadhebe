## Description (Store "Description" field)
Perchboard keeps everything you copy close at hand, without another window in the way.

A slim, quiet shelf rests against the edge of your screen. Move your cursor to it, or press your shortcut, and your recent text, links, images and files slide into view. Click to paste, drag into any app, and the shelf tucks itself away again.

Built for people who copy and paste all day: writers, developers, designers, students and anyone who juggles snippets between apps.

• Always one move away: the shelf lives on the right, left or top edge of your screen and stays out of the way until you need it.
• Every format, neatly sorted: text, links, images with real previews, and files each have their own tab.
• Click to paste or drag and drop: drop images and files straight into chat apps, email, Office and File Explorer.
• Pin what matters: keep templates, addresses and favourite snippets at the top, safe from clearing.
• Instant search: find something you copied days ago in a few keystrokes.
• Private by design: your clipboard history is stored only on your PC and never uploaded. Content that apps mark as sensitive (such as passwords from password managers) is skipped.
• Feels at home on Windows 11: light, dark and system themes, smooth motion and a tiny memory footprint.
• Starts with Windows if you want it to, quietly in the tray.

Perchboard is a one-time purchase. No subscription, no account, no ads.

## Notes for the "Notes for certification" field
Perchboard is a packaged Win32 (Electron) desktop app using runFullTrust, which is needed to read the clipboard and paste into other apps. It lives in the system tray and on the screen edge: after launch, move the mouse to the right edge of the screen (or press Alt+C) to open the clipboard shelf. No account or sign-in is needed. Store builds have no license key and no external payment; the Store handles purchase and updates.
